A job, step by step

Every project, from pitch to approval

Research, concept, moodboard, script, storyboard, handle footage, generate and edit, all connected, all collaborative, all with client feedback embedded.

Brief

Research comes back with a source for each fact, so you can check it before the client does. Draft a few routes from the brief and the brand kit, and the one you pick carries into everything after it.

Script

Picture on the left, sound on the right, and the running time counted as you type. A 15-second Reel and a 60-second film get different beats, and lines from the interviews drop in with their timecode.

Shoot

Crew and call sheets for the day. After the shoot, point the hub at the cards or the drive, and the takes can be searched and played from any browser.

Cut

The client reviews on your link with no login and pins each note to the frame. The last round's notes sit beside the new cut, and their approval moves the card on the board.

FAQ

Questions production companies ask

What is ProductionHUB?

Software for running a video production company: client review, the schedule, crew and call sheets, scripts and storyboards, footage transcripts, quotes, contracts, invoices, time and payroll, in one place, so a job keeps its brief, rushes, client notes and budget together. Listed Creative built it to run its own jobs.

Who is it for?

Video production companies, creative and content agencies, and in-house video teams who are tired of keeping a dozen subscriptions in sync. Freelancers can be given access to just the projects they work on.

Can it replace Frame.io, StudioBinder or Monday?

It covers what most studios use them for: timecoded client review and approval like Frame.io, schedules, crew and call sheets like StudioBinder, and production boards like Monday, plus quotes, e-signature, invoicing, time and payroll. You can start with one part and switch off what you don't need.

Do I have to upload all my footage?

No. The hub makes proxies and transcripts of your rushes in the cloud, so the takes can be searched and played from a browser, while the originals stay on Google Drive, Dropbox or your own storage. Cuts you send for client review are uploaded to the hub.

Do clients need an account to review?

No. Clients review on a link with your logo and no login, pin their notes to the frame and approve from there. Clients who want one can have a portal login that shows only their own productions.

Can clients send us files?

Yes. Clients send files from their portal, and anyone you give an upload link to can send files of up to 50 GB each without a login. You're told when they arrive. Going the other way, you can send masters to the client's portal or on a download link with a password and an end date, and see who downloaded them.

How does the AI handle client work?

It finds, drafts and suggests, and nothing is saved, sent or changed until someone on your team approves it. It uses only providers that don't keep or train on your prompts, and each AI job is charged to its project and the person who ran it.

How much does it cost?

Studio is $29 per seat a month billed annually, or $35 monthly. Studio + Business, which adds sales, quotes, agreements, invoicing, time, leave, payroll export and HR, is $49 per seat a month billed annually, or $59 monthly. Clients, crew and suppliers never need a seat, and you only pay for a seat in months that person uses the hub. 1 TB of storage is included. AI, extra storage and cloud processing are charged at cost from prepaid credit, and each AI cost shows against its project; or connect your own AI keys and pay the providers directly. The free trial runs 14 days and comes with $10 of AI credit.

Can I bring in what we already have?

Yes. Crew lists import from spreadsheets or contacts, schedules from a spreadsheet or a written schedule, and scripts and decks from Word, PDF and PowerPoint files.
