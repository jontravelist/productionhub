From brief to final cut

Every module works from the same project, so nothing is copied from one tool to the next. The research shapes the concept, the concept the script and storyboard, the shoot's transcripts feed the edit, and the client's approval updates the board and the invoice.

Brief

Research reads the web and cites every claim to a page it actually read. Concepts are drafted from the brief, the brand kit and those findings, and the one you pick becomes context for everything after it.

Script

An audio-visual script that knows its format: a 15-second Reel and a 60-second film get different beats, and the runtime is counted as you write. Lines from the interviews drop in with their clip and timecode.

Shoot

Crew and call sheets for the day. After the shoot, point the hub at the cards or the drive and it makes proxies and transcripts in the cloud, so every take can be searched and played from anywhere.

Cut

Clients review on a branded link with no login, leave timecoded notes and draw on the frame. Compare two cuts with the last round's notes beside the new one, and approval moves the card on the board.

One source of truth for every project

Most of what a team needs to decide is already in the project somewhere: in a transcript, a client's email, a note on v2. The hub reads it for you.

Footage management

Proxies and transcripts in the cloud

Point the hub at the cards or the drive and it makes proxies and word-by-word transcripts, with nothing to install. Duplicates are caught first, and the originals stay where they are.

Filed the way your editors work

Bins by day, camera and kind, sorted automatically, with undo. Every take is searchable and plays from any browser, with speakers named once.

Into Premiere and DaVinci Resolve

Save selects, drop them into the script with their source, and send the paper edit to the timeline cut to the word. The Premiere panel brings client notes in as markers too.

Client review and approval, next to the work

Timecoded, drawn on, compared

Notes pinned to a moment or a stretch, drawing on the frame, version stacks, and a compare view that keeps the last round's notes beside the new cut.

Transcripts for clients too

A timecoded transcript of the cut, readable as it plays, downloadable as Word, text or subtitles.

Text removed from the picture

Drag a box over a supered price or a burnt-in caption and the hub erases it, returning the cleaned cut as the next version.

Questions production companies ask

What is ProductionHUB?

Software for running a video production company. Client review and approval, scheduling, crew and call sheets, scripts and storyboards, footage transcripts, quotes, contracts, invoices, time and payroll share one platform, so every job keeps its brief, rushes, client notes and budget together. It was built inside a working production company to run its own projects.

Who is it for?

Video production companies, creative and content agencies, and in-house video teams who are tired of keeping a dozen subscriptions in sync. Freelancers can be given access to just the projects they work on.

Can it replace Frame.io, StudioBinder or Monday?

It covers what most studios use them for: timecoded client review and approval like Frame.io, schedules, crew and call sheets like StudioBinder, and production boards like Monday, plus quotes, e-signature, invoicing, time and payroll. You can also start with one part and switch off the modules you don't need.

Do I have to upload all my footage?

No. The hub makes proxies and transcripts of your rushes in the cloud so every take can be searched and played from a browser, while the original files stay on Google Drive, Dropbox or your own storage. Cuts you send for client review are uploaded to the hub.

Do clients need an account to review?

No. Clients review on a branded link with no login, leave timecoded notes and approve from there. Clients who want one can have a portal login that shows only their own productions, with your logo.

How does the AI handle client work?

As a tool, not the one driving. The AI finds, drafts and suggests, and nothing is saved, sent or changed until someone on your team approves it. It uses only providers that don't store or train on your prompts, and every AI job is costed to the project and the person who ran it.

How much does it cost?

ProductionHUB is priced per seat per month. AI is charged at what the providers charge, from prepaid credit, and every cost shows against its project; or connect your own AI accounts and pay them directly. Studios in the first cohort get founder pricing that stays locked. Request early access and we'll send a quote for your team size.

Can I bring in what we already have?

Yes. Crew lists import from spreadsheets or contacts, schedules from a spreadsheet or a written schedule, and scripts and decks from Word, PDF and PowerPoint files.
