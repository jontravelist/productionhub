Why it's safe on client work

AI that fails closed, rather than making something up.

Every AI feature previews first and saves only on a human's word. When it can't verify something, it refuses — which is unglamorous, and the whole reason you can put this in front of a client.

Preview, then save

Nothing an AI produces is written to your project until a person looks at it and says yes. Every generation, every draft, every import.

Sourced or rejected

Research results are thrown out wholesale if any candidate lacks a real, checkable source. A found email address is discarded unless the name literally appears in the page.

Citations that are checked

The assistant's links are filtered against what its tools actually returned, so it can't cite a project, invoice or document that doesn't exist.

Your numbers, not invented ones

Quotes price off your rate card. Estimates use your line items. Screening uses your rubric. The model does the drafting; your data does the deciding.
